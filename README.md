## What it is

24 business principles, each told through one merchant from the East and one from the West: Fan Li and Vanderbilt, Bai Gui and Cantillon, the Rishengchang draft bank and the Medici, Hu Xueyan and John Law, and more.

Each principle gives what the two merchants did; one line from a primary or early source with its reference and a reliability rating; a short Austrian-economics reading, labelled as one lens and followed by one line from another school; a general "use it today" takeaway; and links to our YouTube videos.

Every quotation was checked character by character against the source texts we saved. The full list of quotes and sources is in [sources/quotes.json](sources/quotes.json).
